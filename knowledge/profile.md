Hanan Zahoor is an AI/ML Engineer and Software Engineer based in Bangalore, India.

He is a Computer Science & Engineering student at Jain University and currently a
Software Development Intern at Deloitte.

His work sits at the intersection of intelligent models and the software that has to
carry them: LLM-powered applications, machine learning pipelines, backend services,
and full-stack products.

On the AI/ML side that means generative AI in practice — LLM APIs, prompt engineering,
agentic workflows, and retrieval over structured and unstructured data. On the
engineering side it means Python and Java backends, REST APIs, automation, and testing.

He is most interested in how systems behave under the hood: where a model's output
stops being trustworthy, where an API quietly breaks down, and what it takes to turn
either into something maintainable.
